Dash (Altrive)

Altrive (also known as Dash, Dakotah, bigman23, dogpond12, Luigi, Pikmin 5) was the channel's third member from 2021-2022 during the Deluxe era.

His first appearance was in the Among Us video Among Us but I'm blindfolded, during the early Deluxe era.

He wouldn't make his first canon appearance until August 25, 2021, with the Mario Maker 2 video Super Peter Griffin World, in which he, Jacob, and Jo embark on the MM2 Super World "Super Peda gifin World". This is also his debut as Luigi.

On January 14th, 2022, in the video Goodbye., Altrive takes his leave from the channel

On November 30, 2023, in The World's Hardest Suika Game, Altrive makes a guest appearance as Jacob, Jo, and many other guests attempt to beat the fanmade Suika-like "Suikarad Gold".
