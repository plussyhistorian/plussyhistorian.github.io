# DotColin

DotColin (aka Colin) was the editor for the channel from the Deluxe era through the entire Platinum era. He served as the third host during the Gold era (alongside Jacob and Jo), as well as the fourth host during the Platinum era (alongside Jacob, Jo, and Ellie).

Aside from being the channel's editor, Colin is most notable for his association with the number 10. This comes from a running bit in which the videos Colin edits always perform at a rank of 10 of 10. Several Mario Party Superstars videos show someone rolling a 10 on the dice, followed by Altrive stating "yo, shoutout DotColin". *(citation needed)* 

Colin is also notable for having been a minor for half of the Deluxe era. This information was kept from Jacob by Altrive, much to Jacob's chagrin.
