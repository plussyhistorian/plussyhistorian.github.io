# Dash (Altrive)
Altrive (also known as Dash, Dakotah, bigman23, dogpond12, Luigi, Pikmin 5) was the channel's third member from 2021-2022 during the Deluxe era.

# History
His first appearance was in the Among Us video [Among Us but I'm Blindfolded](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jo4B7MPqvyg), uploaded on October 4th 2020 (in the early Deluxe era). 

His first canon appearance was on August 25, 2021 with the Mario Maker 2 video [Super Peter Griffin World](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIsrw7er-y0), in which he, Jacob, and Jo embark on the MM2 Super World "Super Peda gifin World". This is also his debut as Luigi.

On January 14th, 2022, in the video [Goodbye.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MQYlEyaq4uc), Altrive leaves the channel. On November 15, 2022, in the video [Altrive Leaves the Channel (real)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9LKGNJ-n_Hk), Altrive leaves the channel for good.

On November 30, 2023, in [The World's Hardest Suika Game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bvYcktPNkp4), Altrive makes a guest appearance while Jacob, Jo, and many other guests attempt to beat the fanmade Suika-like [Suikarad Gold](https://clancywoodburn.itch.io/suikarad-gold).
